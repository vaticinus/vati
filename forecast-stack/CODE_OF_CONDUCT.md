# Code of conduct

Challenge claims and methods, not people's identity or motives. Welcome contributors regardless of background or affiliation. Be specific about evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, credit other people's work, and respect decisions not to share personal information.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, sexualized conduct, spam and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Do not publish private messages, customer records or credentials.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through a verified private channel. Do not put identifying details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban accounts according to severity and repetition. Explain moderation decisions to affected participants where safe; do not expose reporters. A maintainer named in a report must not adjudicate it if another maintainer is available.

Technical disagreement and an unfavorable experimental result are not misconduct. Retaliation against a good-faith report is misconduct. This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, project events and official project channels.
